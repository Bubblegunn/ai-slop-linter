# Measured Paper

The picture is the measurement and nothing else. Every mark on the canvas is a number that exists in a results file, placed by arithmetic, so a reader who opens the evidence folder can find each dot again. There is no illustration, no ornament, no headline set large for effect. Weight comes from position and colour: the arm that matters is the one drawn in ink-blue, and everything else stays in graphite so that the eye goes to it without being told.

Space is honest. An axis that does not start at zero says so in words next to it, and when two panels are shown together they share one scale per point, so the empty stretch on the held-out panel is information: it shows how close every arm sits to the ceiling. Hairlines divide; there are no boxes, no shadows, no cards. The paper is the portfolio's warm off-white, the type is the portfolio's own three faces, each in one role: Bricolage Grotesque names things, Newsreader explains them, JetBrains Mono carries the numbers and the tool's real output.

The craft is in alignment and restraint. Labels hang from one right edge, numbers sit on one tabular column, whisker caps are the same height on every row, and the text that explains the chart is short enough to read in one pass. The work should look like a figure from a careful paper that someone checked line by line, because it was.
